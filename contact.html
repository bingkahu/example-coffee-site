Getting here

Easy to Find, Worth the Trip

🚶

On Foot

We're a 5-minute walk from Maplewood High Street. Head down Brewers Yard, turn left onto Roast Lane — you'll smell us before you see us.

🚌

By Bus

Routes 14, 22, and 47 stop on Maplewood High Street. Walk down Church Passage and we're on the right-hand side.

🚲

By Bike

Plenty of bike racks outside our door. Maplewood is on the National Cycle Network Route 6, so drop in mid-ride!

🚗

By Car

Nearest car park is the Market Square multi-storey (MW1 4AR) — 3 minutes on foot. Free on Sundays after 11 am.
