Why choose us

Coffee, Community & Craft

From bean to cup, every detail matters. Here's what makes Grounds & Glory different from the high-street chains.

🌱

Ethically Sourced

Every bean we pour is traceable to its farm. We work directly with growers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, paying fair prices and visiting annually.

🔥

Roasted In-House

Our head roaster Cleo fires up the Probat drum roaster every Wednesday. Beans are never more than 10 days from roast — freshness you can taste.

🏡

A Real Local Spot

No chain vibes, no loyalty-card apps. Just comfy seats, good music, and baristas who know your name and your order by heart.

🧁

Homemade Treats

Our pastries are baked fresh each morning by our in-house baker Marco. Seasonal specials every week — you'll always have a reason to pop in.

🌿

Plant-Based Friendly

Oat, almond, soy, or coconut — all at no extra charge. We believe great milk alternatives shouldn't cost you more. Everyone belongs here.

💻

Work-Friendly Space

Fast, reliable Wi-Fi. Plenty of plug sockets. An "office hours" back room you can book. We get it — some of your best work happens over a flat white.

"Genuinely the best flat white I've had outside of Melbourne. This place has soul. I come in three times a week and I've never had a bad cup."

— Sophie R., Maplewood resident since 2021
