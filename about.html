The beginning

From a Kitchen Table to Roast Lane

It started, as most good things do, with an obsession. Founders Priya Sharma and Tom Rees met during a specialty coffee workshop in London in 2016. Within an hour of pulling their first espresso on a vintage La Marzocca, they both knew they wanted to do this for a living.

Three years later, after stints working at roasteries in Melbourne and Oslo, the pair returned to Priya's hometown of Maplewood with a clear goal: open a coffee shop that took quality as seriously as the city's best cafés, but felt as warm and unpretentious as your best friend's kitchen.

Grounds & Glory opened its doors in March 2019 with a second-hand espresso machine, twelve bar stools, and a sign hand-painted by Tom's mum. The queue on opening day stretched halfway down Roast Lane, and it hasn't really stopped since.

What we believe in

Our Values, Brewed In

These aren't wall-art platitudes. They're the actual principles that shape every decision we make, from how we source our beans to how we train our baristas.

🤝
Transparency at Every Step
We publish what we pay our farmers. You can scan any bean bag to see the exact farm, harvest date, and price we paid.
♻️
Zero Waste by 2026
Spent grounds go to local allotments. Packaging is fully compostable. Single-use plastic has been off our shelves since 2021.
🧑‍🤝‍🧑
A Place for Everyone
We are committed to being an inclusive, welcoming space. No one should feel out of place in a coffee shop.
📚
Always Learning
Monthly cuppings, barista training days, and supplier farm visits keep our team sharp and our passion alive.
